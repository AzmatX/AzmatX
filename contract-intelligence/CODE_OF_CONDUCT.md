# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and constructive in all communication.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Focus on the work, not individuals.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Sharing private or sensitive data without permission.
- Disruptive behavior that blocks team collaboration.

## Enforcement
Report concerns to repository maintainers. Incidents will be reviewed promptly and handled fairly.
